How do you see the world?

WndrKlub encourages children to be curious, to be storytellers, to see the world from a different perspective. WndrKlub is a series of short, funny, wildly imaginative films that catch the moment an idea takes off, when one thing leads to another and the ordinary turns into a story.

It’s all about the frame.

Wndr(er) test poses by Dixie O’Malley, Young Animator of the Year 2024 (2D Animation, 15 to 18).

The Wndr(er) is a creative explorer, and guides our audience watching at home.

He notices what everyone else walks past: the sway of a tree, the reflection in a window, the sound a door makes when it closes slowly. He pauses where others move on, framing the ordinary until it becomes extraordinary.

He has a child’s sense of wonder: endlessly curious, open to anything, and happiest when his imagination runs off ahead of him. He looks at everything from every angle, upside down, close up and far away, just to see what it might become.

Panels become shots. Page turns become edits. Flipbooks become animation.

WndrKlub introduces children to storytelling and filmmaking through the playful visual language of flipbooks, comics and storyboards, blending 2D line drawings with live-action footage. With humour and curiosity, the series explores movement, sequencing and timing in ways that feel tactile, intuitive and immediately accessible. By building stories frame by frame, children discover how images come to life, how pacing shapes a narrative, and how emotion can be conveyed through visual storytelling.

Music

Rhythm, rhyme and playing with time.

A drip is a beat. A creak is a tune. A wobble is a bassline. In WndrKlub, sound and picture play together, speeding up, slowing down, stopping still, until the beat, the tone, the melody and the buzz of a passing bee all lock in and the whole world starts to groove.

Visual and tactile.

WndrKlub has a tactile, handmade look: drawn lines, cut paper, photos, collage and found footage, gathered together, inspiring children at home to create their own stories.

Inspiring future Wndr(er)s

The WndrKlub series aligns perfectly with the Screen Scotland Film and Screen Education curriculum by introducing children to the fundamentals of filmmaking through a hands-on, playful format.

Just as the curriculum emphasises sequencing, visual storytelling and media literacy, our approach teaches young learners to understand how stories are constructed, how movement and emotion are conveyed, and how images and sound work together.

By allowing children to physically build narratives frame by frame, our series encourages creative collaboration, problem-solving and critical thinking, mirroring the curriculum’s goals of fostering both practical skills and cultural understanding.

Sacha Kyle

Creative Director & Producer

Sacha Kyle is an award-winning director, writer and creative producer with over 20 years across animation, live action, comedy, drama and children’s media, including work for BBC Drama, BBC Comedy, BBC Radio and CBeebies. She is a play specialist and education consultant, a WFTV Fellow, and was named an International Children’s Media Fellow by the LSX Jacobs Foundation and New America. She is a BAFTA member and a Screen Ireland assessor.

Giles Lamb

Composer & Sound Artist

Giles Lamb is an award-winning composer and sound designer with over 30 years of work across film, television, animation, games and immersive installations. In children’s media, his work includes The Brilliant World of Tom Gates, winner of a BAFTA Scotland, and Hushabye Lullabye, winner of two RTS Scotland awards. With a background in neuroscience and music psychology, he creates sound and music that invites children to listen, play and imagine.
